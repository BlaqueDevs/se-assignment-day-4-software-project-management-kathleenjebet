## 3. Compare and contrast Agile and Waterfall methodologies. What are the main advantages and disadvantages of each?
Agile is an iterative,flexible and incremental approach to project management that focuses on customer feedback and adaptability to changing requirements while waterfall methodology is a linear and sequential approach to project management wher a phase must be completed before the next.
Agile has a lesss emphasis on documentation while waterfall relies heavily on documentation.
Agile is easy to manage and faster delivery while although it is less predictable in budget and timelines and requires experienced teams that can make quick decisions.
Waterfall  has a clearely defined structure but has less flexibility and is high risk.
